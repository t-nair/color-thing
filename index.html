For best results
  • Plain background, with the garment filling most of the frame.
  • Block colors, stripes and checks work well. Florals, gradients and tie-dye won't separate cleanly.
  • Uneven lighting is fine — color detection ignores it.
  • On model: the largest clothing item is recolored, and skin is excluded. Beige and camel garments are the hard case, since they sit in the same color range as skin.
